# Bibiliya y'Igiheburayo (Tanaki) — ihindurwa mu Kinyarwanda

Ihindurwa rya Bibiliya y'Igiheburayo (Tanaki) mu Kinyarwanda —
umurongo ku murongo, ijambo ku ijambo, twubaha buri nyuguti.

Iyi ni **intebe ya 64** y'umushinga Selah — intebe ya mbere
y'Ikinyarwanda. Ububiko: `selah-rw`.

## Igiheburayo ni cyo cya mbere

Iri hindurwa ririho kugira ngo rikujyane ku mwandiko w'Igiheburayo —
si ukugira ngo rihagarare mu mwanya wawo. Icyo usoma ni Igiheburayo:
inyuguti zacyo, amajwi yacyo, ibimenyetso byacyo. Umurongo
w'Ikinyarwanda uri hasi ni idirishya, si umusimbura. Aho byombi
bitandukanye, ukuri ni ukw'Igiheburayo.

## Icyo iri hindurwa ritari cyo

- **Ntirisimbura Bibiliya yawe.** Gumana Bibiliya yawe, uyisome,
  usenge uyifashishije. Uyu murimo uhagarara iruhande rwayo — ngo
  ukwereke icyo umwandiko w'Igiheburayo uvuga, ijambo ku ijambo. Ni
  ibyo gusa.
- **Si Ikinyarwanda cyiza cyo kunezeza ugutwi.** Ntidushakisha
  interuro inyuze mu matwi. Aho inyuguti y'Igiheburayo n'interuro
  nziza y'Ikinyarwanda bikururana mu byerekezo bibiri, inyuguti ni yo
  itsinda. **Ukuri kuruta uburyohe bwo gusoma.** Ni cyo gituma hari
  ahantu hakomeye, hataryohera — akenshi kubera ko Igiheburayo
  ubwacyo ari gikomeye aho.
- **Nta nyandiko z'umuhinduzi.** Nta busobanuro, nta gitekerezo, nta
  nyigisho byubatswe mu mwandiko. Ibiri ku murongo biri mu
  Giheburayo — usibye amagambo yongewemo, ayo akaba ari muri ⟨⟩.
- **Ntiryarangiye.** Inzira ya mbere ku mategeko yaranyuzemo, kandi
  ukuboko kw'umuntu kuracyagenzura. Amakosa aracyahari. Ni yo mpamvu
  tugutumira: ngwino urebe.

## Izina

Aho inyuguti enye יהוה zihagaze, twandika **Yahwe** — nta zina
ry'icyubahiro, nta jambo rusange rihagarara mu mwanya waryo.

Ntitwandika **Uwiteka** (izina ry'icyubahiro rishyizwe mu mwanya
w'Izina). Ntitwandika **Yehova** (uburyo buvanze inyuguti z'Izina
n'inyajwi z'irindi jambo). Kandi ijambo **Imana** ntiryicara ku ntebe
y'Izina: iryo jambo rifitiye abantu benshi uburemere bukomeye —
ariko ni ijambo rusange, kandi rihagarara mu **nkingi y'umuhamya**,
si mu yacu. Izina risigara aho riri, uko riri — kugira ngo buri gihe
ubone aho rihagaze koko.

## Kuki Yahwe, atari Uwiteka

Bibiliya zose z'Ikinyarwanda ufite mu ntoki zandika *Uwiteka* aho
inyuguti enye — יהוה — zihagaze, incuro zirenga ibihumbi bitanu.
Ariko *Uwiteka* si izina: ni izina ry'icyubahiro. Icyubahiro
ntigihindura izina — gifata umwanya waryo.

Umunyururu wo gusiba wagenze utya: Ikigiriki *Kyrios* (Septante)
n'Igisiriya *Marya* (Peshitta) → Igifaransa *l'Éternel* / *le
Seigneur* n'Icyongereza *the LORD* by'ababwirizabutumwa → maze mu
Kinyarwanda, **Uwiteka**. Iyi ntebe isubiza Izina aho inyuguti enye
zihagaze.

Kandi hano hari itandukaniro rikwiye kumenyekana: *Uwiteka* si
ijambo ry'iminsi yose ry'Ikinyarwanda. Ntiryigera rikoreshwa ku
muntu; ryavukiye muri Bibiliya kandi rikoreshwa gusa mu mwanya
w'Izina. Ni yo mpamvu muri iri hindurwa **nta ntebe rifite na
rimwe** — ntabwo ari ijambo ryemewe ahandi hantu rikaba ryangiwe ku
ntebe imwe gusa. Aho Igiheburayo hagira אדון ku muntu — nyir'urugo,
shebuja, umutware — twandika *shebuja*, *databuja*, *umutware*
cyangwa *umwami*, kandi ni byo koko: ayo magambo ni meza aho
akwiriye.

## Sheoli, si ikuzimu

שאול ni izina ry'ahantu h'abapfuye mu mwandiko w'Igiheburayo.
Twandika **Sheoli** — izina nk'izina. *Ikuzimu* oya: iryo jambo
ritwaye inyigisho umwandiko w'Igiheburayo utavuga kuri iyo ntebe.

## Alefu-tavu — ⟨את⟩

Mu mwandiko w'Igiheburayo, akajambo gato את kaza imbere y'ikigenerwa
gisobanutse. Ntigahindurwa — ni ikimenyetso cy'imiterere. Tugakerekana
gutya: **⟨את⟩**, mu nyuguti z'Igiheburayo, muri ⟨⟩. Kaboneka mu
mirongo igera hafi ku bihumbi birindwi. Izindi Bibiliya zirakasiba;
twe turakerekana, kugira ngo ubone umwandiko wose.

## Amagambo ari muri ⟨⟩

Ibitari mu Giheburayo ariko interuro y'Ikinyarwanda isaba, bijya
muri ⟨⟩ — kandi make uko bishoboka. Uko ni ko ubona n'amaso yawe
icyo umwandiko uvuga n'icyo ikibonezamvugo cy'Ikinyarwanda gisaba.

## Ibyiciro by'amazina n'Izina

Ikinyarwanda kigenda mu byiciro by'amazina: *umu-/aba-*,
*umu-/imi-*, *iki-/ibi-*, *in-*, *uru-*, *aka-*, *ubu-*, *uku-*,
*aha-*. Buri cyiciro gitanga ibyungo by'ubwumvikane ku nshinga, ku
byerekeza no ku byungo by'ubwite.

**Izina ntiryakira akamenyetso k'icyiciro.** Twandika **Yahwe** —
si *Uwayahwe*, si *Umuyahwe*, si *Abayahwe*, si *Uwuyahwe*.
Igiheburayo gitanga inyuguti enye; ntitwongera akajambo imbere
yazo, ntidusanisha Izina n'ijambo ry'icyubahiro.

**Ariko ubwumvikane ku yandi magambo ni ikibonezamvugo, si ugusiba.**
Yahwe akurikiza icyiciro cy'umuntu; inshinga n'ibyungo bihagarara
uko ururimi rubisaba:

- **Yahwe yaravuze** — *ya-* iri ku nshinga, si ku Izina.
- **ijambo rya Yahwe** — *rya* ni irya *ijambo* (icyiciro cya 5).
- **inzu ya Yahwe** — *ya* ni irya *inzu* (icyiciro cya 9).
- **umutima wa Yahwe** — *wa* ni irya *umutima* (icyiciro cya 3).
- **kuri Yahwe**, **na Yahwe**, **kwa Yahwe** — imigereka
  ihagarara ukwayo, ntiyomeka ku Izina.

Ni ko bimeze no kuri **Elohimu**: *Elohimu yaravuze*, *ijambo rya
Elohimu*, *imbere ya Elohimu* — ariko nta na rimwe *Imana Elohimu*,
nta na rimwe *Uwiteka Elohimu*. Ishingiro ry'izina ntirihinduka
kandi nta zina ry'icyubahiro ryomekwaho.

## Imana — inkingi y'umuhamya

Ijambo **Imana** ni ijambo ry'Ikinyarwanda, kandi ni ryiza. Ariko ni
ijambo rusange, si izina. Aho Igiheburayo hagira אלהים, twandika
**Elohimu**; ijambo *Imana* rigumira mu **nkingi y'umuhamya** — riri
aho ngo rigufashe kumva Elohimu icyo ari cyo, ariko ntiryicara ku
ntebe y'Izina cyangwa ku ntebe ya Elohimu. Uko ni ko ubona
itandukaniro riri hagati y'ijambo umwandiko utanga n'ijambo
tuwusobanura.

## Amazina y'abantu n'ay'ahantu

Amazina y'abantu n'ay'ahantu ahagarara mu majwi yayo y'Igiheburayo:
**Moshe** (Mose), **Mitsirayimu** (Egiputa), **Aburahamu**,
**Yitsihaki**, **Yaakovu**, **Yerushalayimu**, **Dawidi**,
**Shelomo**. Amazina y'Uwo Isiraheli isenga — **Yahwe**,
**Elohimu**, **Eli**, **Eloahi**, **Adonayi**, **Shadayi**,
**Tsevaoti**, **Yahi** — yose mu majwi yayo; nta na rimwe risimburwa
n'izina ry'icyubahiro.

## Amagambo yagumye uko ari

Amagambo amwe y'Igiheburayo atwaye uburemere nta jambo rimwe
ry'Ikinyarwanda ryabasha kwikorera ryonyine; ahagarara mu majwi yayo:
**hesedi** (urukundo rw'isezerano), **Sheoli**, **Mashiyah**
(uwasizwe amavuta), **Tora**, **Shema**, **Shabati**, **koheni**
(umutambyi), **navi** (umuhanuzi).

## Buri murongo mu rumuri rwawo

Itegeko ry'uyu murimo: buri murongo wandikwa uri mu mwanya
w'uwawusomye bwa mbere. Amagambo yo mu Ntangiriro 22:1 ntazi
Intangiriro 22:13. Umwandiko uvugira mu gihe cyawo.

## Uko dosiye ziteye

Umurongo umwe — dosiye imwe: umurongo ubwawo, hiyongereyeho
isobanuro rimwe kuri buri gace k'ijambo, iruhande rw'ijambo ryako
ry'Igiheburayo — kugira ngo buri jambo ry'Igiheburayo risomerwe mu
maso y'Ikinyarwanda. Dosiye iteye gutya:
`<igitabo>/<igice>/<umurongo>.json`.

## Shyigikira Selah

<https://selahproject.com>

---

**English summary.** The Selah Kinyarwanda Rendering — chair 64, the
first Kinyarwanda chair. A letter-faithful, verse-by-verse and
word-by-word rendering of the Hebrew Bible into standard Kinyarwanda.

Where the four-letter Name stands we write **Yahwe** — never
*Uwiteka* (the Bible-only Lord-title that every Kinyarwanda Bible
puts in the Name's seat), never *Yehova* (the hybrid), and never
*Imana* at the Name seat. *Imana* is a good Kinyarwanda word and it
keeps its place, but it is a common noun: it stands in the **witness
column**, explaining what Elohim is, never on the Name's chair.
אלהים is **Elohimu**. שאול is **Sheoli**, never *ikuzimu* — that
word carries a theology the Hebrew does not speak at that seat. Every
direct-object את appears as **⟨את⟩**; supplied words appear only
inside ⟨⟩, as few as possible.

**The noun-class posture.** Kinyarwanda is a Bantu language built on
noun classes, and the rule at this chair is a split one: **the Name
takes no class prefix** — never *Uwayahwe*, *Umuyahwe*, *Abayahwe*,
*Uwuyahwe*; the Hebrew gives four letters and we do not weld a class
marker onto the front of them — **but agreement on the other words is
grammar, not erasure**. *Yahwe yaravuze* (the *ya-* is on the verb),
*ijambo rya Yahwe* (class 5 concord on *ijambo*), *inzu ya Yahwe*
(class 9), *umutima wa Yahwe* (class 3), *kuri / na / kwa Yahwe* —
all correct. The stem stays intact; the concord belongs to the word
it agrees with, not to the Name. The same holds for **Elohimu**.

Note on *Uwiteka*: unlike the Lord-words of other chairs, *Uwiteka*
is not everyday vocabulary that is merely refused at one seat — it is
purely biblical, coined for the Name's chair, and so it has **no seat
at all** in this rendering. For a human אדון we write *shebuja*,
*databuja*, *umutware* or *umwami*, and those are right.

Precision outranks readability; the Hebrew is always right where the
two differ. One verse per file
(`<book>/<chapter>/<verse>.json`), one gloss per word-unit.
