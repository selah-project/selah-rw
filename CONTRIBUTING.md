# Gutanga umusanzu mu ihindurwa rya Selah mu Kinyarwanda

Basomyi, abiga Igiheburayo, abahinduzi n'abakuriye mu Kinyarwanda —
murakaza neza. Muzi uru rurimi kurusha imashini. Iyi nyandiko
ivuga uko wadufasha.

## Kumenyesha ikosa, gutanga igitekerezo

- Fungura **Issue** mu bubiko:
  <https://github.com/selah-project/selah-rw>
- Cyangwa, niba ushobora kurikosora ubwawe, fungura **Pull request**.
- Cyangwa utwandikire: <support@selahproject.com>

## Ibigomba kuba muri ubwo butumwa

- Aho biri: igitabo, igice, umurongo (urugero, `genesis 1:1`).
- Icyanditse aho ubu n'impamvu ari amakosa.
- Icyagombye kwandikwa, niba ubizi.

## Amategeko yo gukosora dosiye

- Dosiye imwe = umurongo umwe:
  `<igitabo>/<igice>/<umurongo>.json`.
- **Umubare w'uduce tw'amagambo ungana n'umubare w'ibisobanuro**:
  buri gace k'ijambo ry'Igiheburayo gahabwa isobanuro rimwe gusa;
  ntugabanye, ntwongere.
- **Ibimenyetso ⟨את⟩ birahoraho** — ntibisibwe, ntibyongerwe.
- Amazina ahagarara nk'uko D1 ibivuga (reba
  `docs/methodology/translation-discipline/rw.md` mu bubiko
  nyamukuru): **Yahwe**, **Elohimu**, **Eli**, **Eloahi**,
  **Adonayi**, **Shadayi**, **Tsevaoti**, **Yahi**. Izina
  ry'icyubahiro ntirihagarara mu mwanya w'Izina — nta *Uwiteka*,
  nta *Yehova*, nta *Imana* ku ntebe y'Izina cyangwa ku ya Elohimu.
- **Izina ntiryakira akamenyetso k'icyiciro**: si *Uwayahwe*, si
  *Umuyahwe*, si *Abayahwe*, si *Uwuyahwe*. Ariko ubwumvikane ku
  yandi magambo ni ikibonezamvugo, si ugusiba: *Yahwe yaravuze*,
  *ijambo rya Yahwe*, *inzu ya Yahwe*, *umutima wa Yahwe*, *kuri
  Yahwe* — byose ni byo. Ishingiro ry'Izina risigara ridahinduwe.
- שאול ni **Sheoli**, si *ikuzimu*.
- Ijambo ryongewemo rijya muri ⟨⟩ gusa, kandi ari uko interuro
  y'Ikinyarwanda ibisabye.
- Nta jambo ry'Icyongereza cyangwa ry'Igifaransa risigara mu
  murongo — *the*, *and*, *God*, *LORD*, *le*, *la*, *Dieu*,
  *Seigneur* n'andi — na muri ⟨⟩.

## Igipimo

Ukuri ku nyuguti kuruta uburyohe bwo gusoma. Niba igikosora
gitumye interuro iryohera ariko kikatuvana ku nyuguti
y'Igiheburayo, ntikinjira. Niba gituma iba nyayo kurushaho,
turagusaba: kizane.

---

**English.** Contributions welcome: open an Issue or Pull request at
<https://github.com/selah-project/selah-rw>, or write to
<support@selahproject.com>. One file = one verse; unit count equals
gloss count; ⟨את⟩ markers are never deleted or added; the D1 Names
(Yahwe, Elohimu…) never yield to a title — not *Uwiteka*, not
*Yehova*, not *Imana* at the Name or Elohim seat. The Name takes
**no noun-class prefix** (never *Uwayahwe*, *Umuyahwe*, *Abayahwe*,
*Uwuyahwe*), but class agreement on the surrounding words is grammar,
not erasure — *Yahwe yaravuze*, *ijambo rya Yahwe*, *inzu ya Yahwe*,
*umutima wa Yahwe*, *kuri Yahwe* are all correct and the stem stays
intact. שאול is **Sheoli**, never *ikuzimu*; supplied words only in
⟨⟩; no English or French words survive in the line, brackets
included. Letter-faithfulness outranks readability.
