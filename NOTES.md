## The Uwiteka erasure

The word every Kinyarwanda Bible prints at the Name appeared in the
store's whole life exactly **twice** in token rows: 2 Sam 5:2 (the
first hit, caught at burn-watch) and 2 Chr 7:6 (both Name seats of
one verse, caught by the Tekoa fork). Uhoraho and Nyagasani appeared
only as fabrications at non-Name surfaces (בית, השלמים, the particle
נא) — never displacing יהוה. **The Name seat itself was never
shadowed by the national Bible's vocabulary** — the cleanest
Latin-home erasure record since the polarity was named.

## The topology inversion (the chair's crux)

Kinyarwanda welds noun-class prefixes onto nearly every noun. The
rails inverted the topology: the Name stands bare, the grammar lands
on the words *around* it. At the seal: **zero class-prefix or augment
welds on Yahwe or Elohimu across 23,213 files** — no *Uwayahwe*, no
*Umuyahwe*, no *Iyahwe* (one I-weld at 2 Kgs 18:25 was restored at
seating) — while ~6,800 lawful agreement forms (kwa Yahwe, rya Yahwe,
Elohimu wacu) carry the grammar. The associative particle set held
(wa/ya/rya/cya/bya/za/bwa/rwa/kwa; the lone *lya* and two *kya*
drift forms were restored).

**The nti-weld class (NEW):** the verbal negative prefix nti- grabbed
the Name twice (*ntiYahwe azaba* at Num 14:43; *Ese ntiYahwe njye* at
Isa 45:21) — the negation was moved onto the verb where Kinyarwanda
puts it (Yahwe ntazaba; si njye Yahwe). Watch this class at every
Bantu chair.

**The lowercase-uhoraho lawful class (NEW):** *uhoraho / ihoraho /
gihoraho* — verbal forms of guhoraho, "to endure" — are ordinary
vocabulary at non-divine surfaces (עתק Prov 8:18, נצחת Jer 8:5,
וקים Dan 6:27). The erasure title and the living verb share a
skeleton; the sweep must stay token-guided, never stem-gleaned.
